With Kiro installed, the last setup step is signing in. Kiro supports several
providers. If youpick whichever your account or organization uses:

- **GitHub** — sign in with your GitHub account.
- **Google** — sign in with your Google credentials.
- **AWS Builder ID** — quick setup for individual developers.
- **AWS IAM Identity Center** — enterprise sign-in (see below).
- **External identity provider** — your org's IdP, e.g. Microsoft Entra ID or Okta.

<Aside type="note">
Individual methods (GitHub, Google, Builder ID) aren't available in AWS GovCloud
(US) regions — there you'll use IAM Identity Center or an external IdP.
</Aside>

<Tabs syncKey="kiro-surface">
  <TabItem label="IDE">
    <Steps>

    1. On first launch, Kiro shows the sign-in screen. Choose your provider.

    2. You're redirected to your browser to complete sign-in, then sent back to
       Kiro automatically.

    3. Once signed in, you'll land on the welcome page and can open a project.

    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="CLI">
    <Steps>

    1. Start sign-in from your terminal:

       ```bash
       kiro-cli login
       ```

    2. Press **Enter** to open your browser, then choose your provider (Google,
       GitHub, Builder ID, or your organization).

    3. After authenticating, the browser directs you back to your terminal and
       the CLI detects the login automatically.

    4. Confirm who you're signed in as:

       ```bash
       kiro-cli whoami
       ```

       To sign out later, run `kiro-cli logout`.

    </Steps>

    <Aside type="tip" title="Signing in on a remote machine">
    Over SSH, SSM, or in a container the CLI can't open a browser. Run
    `kiro-cli login` and it shows a URL plus a one-time code — open the URL on
    any device, enter the code, and the CLI detects the login. No port
    forwarding required. (External IdP login isn't supported via device flow yet.)
    </Aside>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## AWS IAM Identity Center

If your organization uses IAM Identity Center, you'll need two things from your
admin or help desk: a **Start URL** and the **Region** that hosts your identity
directory.

<Steps>

1. Choose **Sign in with AWS IAM Identity Center**.

   {/* SCREENSHOT: IDE/CLI provider selection with "AWS IAM Identity Center" highlighted.
       Drop the image in src/assets/setup/ and replace this comment with an <Image /> import. */}

2. In **Start URL**, enter the URL provided by your admin
   (e.g. `https://my-org.awsapps.com/start`).

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Start URL entry field filled in. */}

3. In **Region**, enter the AWS Region hosting the identity directory, then
   choose **Continue** and finish sign-in in your browser.

   {/* SCREENSHOT: Region field + Continue button. */}

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="AWS GovCloud (US)">
A GovCloud Start URL contains `us-gov-home`, e.g.
`https://start.us-gov-home.awsapps.com/directory/d-XXXXXXXXXX`. IAM Identity
Center routing is automatic, but GovCloud requires **Kiro IDE 0.9.2+** or
**Kiro CLI 1.25.0+**.
</Aside>
